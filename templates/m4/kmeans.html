K-Means++ Initialization & Choosing K

1. K-Means++ Seeding: Rather than uniformly random starting centroids (which frequently traps Lloyd's algorithm in suboptimal local minima), K-Means++ chooses subsequent centroids with probability proportional to the squared Euclidean distance from the nearest existing centroid: D(x)² / ∑ D(x')².
2. Elbow Method: Inertia measures within-cluster sum of squared errors. The inflection point (elbow) occurs at K = 4.
3. Silhouette Metric: For sample i with mean intra-cluster distance a(i) and nearest-cluster distance b(i), s(i) = (b(i) - a(i)) / max(a(i), b(i)), yielding a strong cluster quality score of 0.34.
