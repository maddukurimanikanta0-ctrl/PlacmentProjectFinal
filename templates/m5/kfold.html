Nested Cross-Validation for Unbiased Evaluation

When tuning hyperparameters (e.g. tree depth or regularization α), evaluating on the same cross-validation loop introduces optimistically biased estimates.

Nested CV Architecture:
• Inner Loop (K=3): Selects optimal hyperparameters across validation folds.
• Outer Loop (K=5): Evaluates the model with chosen hyperparameters on completely unseen outer test folds, ensuring truly unbiased generalization estimation.
