Hyperparameter Search Paradigms Comparison

Why random search and Bayesian optimization consistently outperform exhaustive grid search:

Search Strategy Exploration Mechanism Sample Efficiency Effective Hyperparameter Dimension
Grid Search Exhaustive Cartesian product over discrete grid Poor (wastes compute evaluating uninfluential parameters) Fixed grid resolution; suffers curse of dimensionality
Random Search (Bergstra-Bengio) Uniform random sampling from continuous probability densities High (efficiently explores distinct values per dimension) Discovers near-optimal points in 10x fewer iterations
Bayesian Optimization (Optuna TPE) Tree-structured Parzen Estimators modelling P(param | score) Highest (balances exploration of uncertainty vs exploitation) Adaptive sequential define-by-run trial guidance
