SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) Preview

Unlike heuristic feature importance scores, SHAP values are rooted in cooperative game theory and guarantee local accuracy:

f(x) = E[f(x)] + ∑ φi(x)

Every prediction is decomposed into the base expected placement rate plus individual feature attribution weights (φi), explaining exactly why a given student was predicted Placed or Not Placed!
