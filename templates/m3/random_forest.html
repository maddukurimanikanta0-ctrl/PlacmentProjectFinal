Mathematical Foundation of Variance Reduction

For an ensemble of B individual trees each having variance σ² with average pairwise correlation ρ, the total ensemble variance is given by:

Var(f̄) = ρ • σ² + [ (1 - ρ) / B ] • σ²

By randomly subsampling m = √p features at each split, Random Forests decrease the pairwise tree correlation ρ, driving total ensemble variance down far more effectively than standard bagging!
