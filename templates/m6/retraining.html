Continuous Retraining Strategies
Strategy Trigger Condition Compute Cost Best Applied To
Scheduled Retraining Calendar cadence (e.g. Monthly / End of Semester) Predictable recurring cost Seasonal student cohorts with stable curriculum standards
Drift-Triggered Retraining Event-driven (PSI > 0.25 on entrance exam distributions) Pay-per-drift optimal compute Sudden shifts in college admissions or grading criteria
Performance-Triggered Retraining Ground-truth feedback (True Placement rate decays below 90%) Requires delayed label ingestion Post-placement season audit and recalibration
Shadow Deployment (Safe Verification)

Incoming production inference traffic is mirrored asynchronously to the newly retrained candidate model (v2.4). Responses from candidate model are logged and compared for latency and divergence, but only champion model responses are returned to users.

Canary Deployment (Progressive Rollout)

Route 10% of real student traffic to the new model. If error rates remain < 0.05% and latency percentiles are within SLA over 48 hours, traffic is sequentially promoted to 25%, 50%, and 100%.
